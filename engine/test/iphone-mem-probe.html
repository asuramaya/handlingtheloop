How to read it
  1. Tap Allocate (or Auto-step) and watch Currently held climb.
  2. When the page suddenly blanks & reloads, that's the kill. The verdict shows the size that died.
  3. That number is this device's per-page ceiling. Subtract a margin → the seatbelt budget.
  4. Run it once on a blank load (device ceiling), then later inside the app (headroom). ceiling − headroom = app baseline.

int16 chunks, every 4 KB page touched so iOS actually commits them (untouched allocations don't count).
