Handling The Loop

A DJ rig that runs in a browser tab.

Two decks, four-lane stem separation, a real effects rack, hardware and gamepad control, and a room your friends can walk into while you play. Nothing to install.

Open the decks Free. Works on desktop Chrome today; phones ride along.

Stems, on device

Split any track into drums, bass, vocals and instruments — then EQ, filter and mangle each lane on its own chain.

A rack that means it

Parametric EQ, multiband saturation, an FDN reverb, compression, and pad-thrown effects — all of it real DSP, not presets over a filter.

Play together

Share one link. Friends join the same session from their own devices, or listen in while you run the set.

Your gear works

USB-MIDI controllers, MIDI-Learn for anything unmapped, a second output for headphone cue, and a mic input with talkover ducking.
